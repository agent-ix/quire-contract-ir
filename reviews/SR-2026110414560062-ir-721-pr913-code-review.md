## Evidence

`Walk::edge` charges before `seen_edges.insert`; a repeated `Reference` from one reached term to the same target produces the same `(from, TermReference, target)` key but charges twice. The visited set controls enqueueing only. The AC and behavior require resolving each directed edge once. Add a value-asserting repeated-reference fixture and move the charge to the first visit.

`visit_selected_edges` bulk-pushes every `Application` argument or `Aggregate` member via `pending.extend` and gathers every nominal target in a new `Vec` before the visitor can stop at its allowance. A wide reached term with a low remaining limit therefore performs uncharged inspection and allocation proportional to the complete child list. Use an iterator-frame stack or charge before materializing each child, and exercise wide as well as deep terms.

The strict fixture calls `CheckedPackageV2::read` and requires `Admitted` before selecting its monitor root. Its foreign and specialized adverse cases explicitly mutate retained terms after admission, as AC-12 states. No reviewer aggregate gate was run.
