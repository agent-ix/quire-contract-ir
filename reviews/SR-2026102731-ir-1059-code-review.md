## Review evidence

QSpec FR-322's step 4 table assigns native Duration the selected owner's resolved s unit type, Quantity<s>, subject to the stated unit checks. TK-25 and TK-26 explicitly admit Duration in tuple positions and variant payloads. This PR recognizes Duration in `NativeTypeRef`, and its intake loop admits it, but `DomainModel::element_type_leaf` returns `None` for Duration. `check_tuple_position` and `selected_union_members` use `element_type` and therefore cannot validate an otherwise valid projection or union value with that type. The replay tests only step 1 admission for TK-25/26 and remove expression nodes, so they do not reach either use.
