With the variable unset or empty, or pointing at /tmp, the target fails (exit 101, a test failure naming the variable). It does not skip.
  - tests/conformance_qspec has `test = false`, and the `make test` log has no conformance_qspec run.

## Verdict

Every AC in scope is traced to a non-vacuous test. 23 bindings were checked and all are correct; no trace is wrong.

- FND-001 is medium and is not a defect of this PR. It is the measured QSL break, which FR-038 already declares. Route it to QSL: one map_refusal_code arm, four NOT_EMITTED_BY_QSL entries, and the UnsupportedConstruct arm dropped on PR #616. No QSL ticket exists yet.
- FND-002 through FND-008 are low. Each flags a reading where the spec is silent, or where the spec text lags behind the fixture.
- FND-009 is a low coverage gap in AC-102.

The AC-103 unit test that calls validate_temporal directly is the right observation point, because the dependency join makes the case unreachable through a package read. The AC should say so (FND-008).
