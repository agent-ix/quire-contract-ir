A clock-configured package with one temporal clause and two `protocol`/`compensation` nodes admits three `temporal_bindings` rows (`CB-01`), and a package with two compensations and no clause admits two (`CB-02`). The borrowed reader returns the exact admitted declaration ids, names and closed configurations without a per-compensation profile law.
